Keep it consistent

Preserve the aspect ratio and the complete face. Keep clear space of about one pupil diameter around the mark in print and marketing layouts. Use a single ink and keep CRT glow and scanlines separate from the artwork.

SVG is preferred for print. Check a physical proof at the intended size before commercial printing. The provided PNGs carry 300ppi metadata.
